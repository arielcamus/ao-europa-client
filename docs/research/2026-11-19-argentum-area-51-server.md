# AOSpain and the WarroS clan (Argentum Online, Spain)

**Answer: AOSpain was real. It was a Spanish custom Argentum Online server announced in 2003 (aospain.com, built on AO 0.9.9c with its own client "AOSpain 1.0"), but it was dead or for sale by 2005-2007, so it probably is NOT where WarroS played in its main years.** WarroS was a Spain-based clan whose own site (warros.es) is archived 2009-2012 and lists AODrag, ImperiumAO, AOMania/Argentumania and Tierras Perdidas as its servers, never AOSpain. Either Ariel's server is one of those, an earlier WarroS era on AOSpain, or a later namesake.
**Cheapest next test:** ask Ariel when he played (year) and which character/staff name he remembers. 2003-05 fits AOSpain; 2006-10 fits AODrag/ImperiumAO/AOMania, where the WarroS forum has per-server member lists.

(Pass 3 of the research, ~9 min wall-clock for passes 2 and 3. Earlier passes: Area 51 and Area 53, all empty, see appendix.)

## 1. AOSpain (aospain.com), what the archive shows
- **Ran:** announcements dated May 2003. Site copyright "AOSpain 2003-04" (the home page footer, VERIFIED). Wayback has about 55 snapshots of aospain.com from 28 May 2003 to Aug 2010, then a later unrelated Chinese spam site (2020s). VERIFIED: http://web.archive.org/web/20030528042308/http://www.aospain.com/
- **Who:** news posts are signed "Neptuno" (admin/staff voice, VERIFIED). A 2004 splash page credits "AOSPain 2004" and the footer later says "InpKill Corp." (VERIFIED, https://web.archive.org/web/20031124001806/http://www.aospain.com/). The staff page (staff.php) is not archived, so the full staff list is UNCONFIRMED.
- **What it was:** a non-official, modified AO. The May 2003 page says: first beta of "client 1.0" of AOSpain, "exclusive to our server"; a test server before the definitive one; a new life/exp system (shows monster life, gives exp as a percent of damage); alchemy and tailoring almost done; ~300 base graphics re-indexed and "many renewed graphics to mark the difference"; new maps ("Nuevo Dungeon Newbie", Ciudad de Tempano, Cueva del Norte, Piramides, Nuevo Infierno); "sagrado" (sacred) items; new weapons and amulets; plans to merge official 0.9.9.10 (-33% bandwidth). Downloads: AO 0.9.9c server source and map editor from ao.alkon.com.ar. VERIFIED (same URL).
- **Community:** IRC channel #AOSpain on IRC-Hispano (staff there 24h), a forum, a "Servidores" section for unofficial servers, a clans section (all in the 2003 menu). Dev files hosted at desarrollodragon.com/aospain (map editor, patch). Host in May 2003 changed after "problems with the old hosting". VERIFIED (same URL).
- **How it ended:** 2004 splash "AOSPain 2004"; Oct 2005 page reads "AOSpain Siempre en el Recuerdo" (in memory of, i.e. closed); June 2007 page says "Aospain en venta" with an [REDACTED EMAIL] contact (domain for sale). VERIFIED: http://web.archive.org/web/20051028112844/http://www.aospain.com/ and http://web.archive.org/web/20070609183638/http://www.aospain.com/. Reason for closure not found (UNCONFIRMED).
- **Size:** no player counts found. UNKNOWN.
- **Related:** aospain.tk redirect domain (snapshots 2003-2005 and 2008, only a stub, VERIFIED http://web.archive.org/web/20080612094331/http://www.aospain.tk:80/). A GitHub repo https://github.com/scorpio21/AoSpain (2026, 32-bit VB6 engine, 10 chars per account, active in March 2026) reuses the name; it appears to be a new project, not the 2003 server (ESTIMATED, no link between them found).
- **Gaps:** inner pages (staff, historial, rules, news) and snapshots from Nov 2003 to 2005 returned empty for me, probably Wayback rate limiting. Worth retrying later for the staff list and player numbers. Client download links were not archived as far as I saw.

## 2. WarroS clan
- **Site:** warros.es, "Pagina oficial del clan WarroS - Argentum Online", run from Spain, admin "Morpheo". Forum on phpBB with 1,474 registered users and 15,635 posts, peak 13 users online (13 Mar 2006). VERIFIED: http://web.archive.org/web/20100924045217/http://www.warros.es/foro/index.php
- **Servers it covered, by forum section (VERIFIED, same URL):** AODrag (oldest sections, last activity 2006-2010), ImperiumAO (private zones dated 2008), AOMania / "Argentumania espanol" (candidates up to Sep 2010), plus the web games Bite Fight, OGame and Shoguns Fate, and an "Ao Alkon" section. Moderators/leaders named: Kumbaya, YsiOn, Morpheo; posters include TaPiOn, FelucO, Salot, Malashae, Javier, Xiwi, Dante, Yondaime.
- **Timeline:** Morpheo's post 10 Dec 2009 "Caduca la web" says the hosting lapses on 27 Dec, the forum has had almost no posts for a year and "as far as I know the whole clan plays no server"; the site was nonetheless still up in 2010-2012. Home page 2012 still has Spanish AO news: http://web.archive.org/web/20120411212333/http://www.warros.es/ . The site is dead today (DNS does not resolve).
- **Other:** warros.com and warros.tk have Wayback snapshots but looked like parked pages (UNCONFIRMED). A YouTube video "Cancion Clan WarroS 2" by Leandro Sosa (Aug 2020) came up in search and is likely a different clan (UNCONFIRMED, not opened).
- **No AOSpain mention found** on any warros.es page I could read (home 2009-2012, forum index 2010).
